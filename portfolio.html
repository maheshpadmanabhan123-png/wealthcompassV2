    How it works

    Set it. Then leave it alone.

    The best portfolio management is boring on purpose. Here is the whole loop.

    01

    Choose the risk you can live with

    Not the highest return on a slide — the mix you will still hold in a bad year. We test that with real scenarios, not a checkbox.

    02

    We build and watch

    Global diversification, low-cost funds and daily drift checks. You get a notification only when something needs you.

    03

    Rebalance with taxes in mind

    Small, deliberate trades — never a reaction to headlines. Where it makes sense, we use new money instead of selling.

    Local rules, handled

    The same plan. Different paperwork.

    Tax wrappers are not a detail. They are the difference between what your portfolio earns and what you keep.

    ELSS & 80C

    Tax-saving equity funds with a three-year lock-in. Useful — as long as the lock-in fits your goal, not just your tax bill.

    Tax-saving3-year lock-in

    PPF & EPF

    The quiet backbone of many Indian retirements. Slow, safe and tax-efficient — best treated as the stable part of the plan.

    SafeLong-term

    NPS

    A retirement-specific account with an extra deduction and a long horizon. Compass factors in the lock-in and annuity rules.

    RetirementExtra deduction

    End-of-service gratuity

    A real retirement asset that many people spend by accident. Compass plans it as part of the long-term picture.

    RetirementOften overlooked

    DIFC & ADGM plans

    Workplace savings plans in the free zones. The rules differ from onshore arrangements, so the model treats them separately.

    WorkplaceFree zone

    No personal income tax

    That changes the maths — but not the need for diversification. Many UAE portfolios are overexposed to property and local banks.

    Rebalancing

    Maintenance, not market timing.

    When one part of the portfolio runs ahead, the plan quietly sells a little and tops up what lagged. No forecast required.
